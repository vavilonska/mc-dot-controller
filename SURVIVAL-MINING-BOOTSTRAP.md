# Bounded survival-mining bootstrap

The public mine_local capability now supports exact-axis oak, spruce, birch,
jungle, acacia and dark-oak logs, plus iron ore and deepslate iron ore. Its
original twelve targets remain unchanged. The public schema, skill, native
admission/footprint and strict Resident witness consumer all use the extension.

Exact native vanilla identities and classes remain required. Log axis must be
one of x/y/z and match the observed state. Iron ores have no properties. The
normal correct-tool check remains enabled by default. The target cannot be in
the player's own support column; the player must remain stationary and safely
supported. No navigation, tool switching, pickup or replay is added.

Natural context is explicit, not a general unknown-block bypass. Dry ordinary
leaves, grass/ferns, selected flowers and vines are read and rechecked with their
exact properties, classes and collision constraints. Harmless collision-empty
plants may occupy body cells, but vines and leaves cannot. Leaves cannot support
the player. Any footprint state change, including natural leaf distance/decay
or lost vine support, still stops; an attempted/uncertain action is never replayed.

Coal extraction is deliberately unavailable because its XP orbs are outside the
current mining entity policy. Coal ores may be neighboring context. The initial
fuel path is carried fuel or separately crafted/smelted charcoal. Bamboo and
unknown geometry remain blocked even in a bamboo-jungle spawn. Moving to an
ordinary dry supported patch is required; this patch does not create one.

Placement imports have been separated onto the original frozen support sets.
No placement vocabulary is broadened as a side effect of these mining changes.

See client-mod/validation/survival-mining/README.md for offline evidence scope.
The extension has no live-world or native-gameplay acceptance claim.
