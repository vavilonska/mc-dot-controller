# Managed dry-step checks

`ManagedDryStepPolicyProbe.java` exercises the pure helper without Minecraft or Baritone. The source has not been compiled or run by the water/API inspection worker; coordinate the serialized JVM slot before compilation or execution.

The native adapter is responsible for exact current executor/path/movement identity, source freshness, same owner/world/player binding, loaded-cell snapshots, ordinary gravity and jumping (`noGravity=false`, standard gravity/jump/effects), exact dry friction 0.6, no sprint or movement boosts, and a final input/movement fence. It separately proves current horizontal speed at most 0.35 and player speed at most 0.100001, then adds at most one exact yaw-bound ordinary movement impulse with an upward-rounded magnitude bound of 0.101. This accounts for vanilla's float `0.21600002 / (0.6 * 0.6 * 0.6)`; friction below 0.6 is not admitted because it increases this acceleration. `Motion` carries a conservative velocity endpoint. The two-motion overloads receive `maxImpulse` (current delta plus the maximum yaw-bound impulse) and `noImpulse` (original delta), both with the same conservative jump component. This brackets every actual acceleration amount from 0 through 0.101, including weaker airborne input opposing the current velocity. Both endpoints are independently validated; existing one-motion signatures pass that same motion as both endpoints. Its vertical component must be at least 0.42 when an ordinary grounded jump is pending. The helper does not substitute for those proofs.

`assessStation(Box, Motion, CellReader)` is a separate admission or neutral path-calculation check. It uses the actual current body footprint and the same conservative coasting margin, with no invented adjacent waypoint. It requires feet at a full-block support elevation and validates the support directly below them. The native caller must separately prove grounded. A missing current path may only wait with neutral intent; this helper does not authorize movement without a proven step.

The helper admits same-level cardinal and diagonal steps and cardinal one-block up/down. It checks the exact swept actual horizontal body box toward the destination center, plus the full axis-aligned reachable rectangle enclosing the actual center and both 11.2-times velocity endpoints (`maxImpulse` and `noImpulse`). Both use the actual body half extents with 0.10 extra clearance on each side. The coast rectangle covers collisions clipping either horizontal component independently, including during an ascent; it does not add an unrelated neighborhood. The multiplier exceeds `1 / (1 - 0.91)` and covers the longer coast during ordinary airborne descent. Post-impulse horizontal speed is bounded at 0.451 blocks per tick. At most 81 horizontal candidates and 128 sampled cells are permitted; an over-budget envelope fails closed. Every touched column needs a dry full support at the source or destination support elevation and a loaded dry scan through the higher of planned clearance and the complete remaining neutral-input jump apex. The vertical rise starts from the larger endpoint Y component, sums each positive `vy`, then updates `vy = (vy - 0.08) * 0.98`, for at most 32 iterations, and adds 0.01 blocks for floating-point margin. A still-positive value after the iteration bound fails closed. Every sampled cell above the chosen support must have clear body collision, including at least two cells and any higher jump envelope. A proven higher full support seals off lower cells, so water beneath a dry solid floor is not mistaken for route water.

The probe covers normal dry movement, dry steps and bridges over water, stationary admission and full-jump clearance, shallow/flowing/waterlogged or deep destinations, diagonal corner and momentum exposure, off-segment water reachable after independent horizontal component clipping, opposing-velocity water hidden by a maximum-input sign reversal, airborne descent coast beyond the former three-times margin, water or a high solid roof in the upward head envelope, a roof above the one-tick envelope but within the full 0.42 jump apex, the 0.451 accepted/0.452 refused speed boundary, the 128-cell fail-closed budget, read failures, bounds, unknown cells, unsupported step geometry, and input nonfiniteness. Tests are ordinary Java `main` checks and require only the helper source.

## Pinned path-reader scope

`ManagedDryRoute` admits verified `baritone.bx` paths through exact pinned Guava `Lists$RandomAccessReverseList` positions with an exact `ArrayList` `forwardList`, and `baritone.dn` paths with exact `ArrayList` positions and movements. It reads the raw backing fields and does not invoke list-wrapper callbacks. `baritone.dl` cutoff paths are explicitly refused with `managed_navigation_cutoff_path_reader_unsupported`; their JDK unmodifiable/sublist wrapper chain has no admitted callback-free reader.

Ordinary fully loaded, verified goal-reaching paths remain `bx` and are supported. The default static cutoff (factor 0.9, minimum length 30) only produces `dl` when the path does not reach the goal and meets the length threshold; goal-reaching paths bypass it. `dl` can also result from an unloaded-boundary cutoff (`cutoffAtLoadBoundary` defaults false but is enabled by MDC managed navigation), post-processing reconstruction truncation, or executor history trimming after the default 300-position threshold (removing 50 positions). These truncated/boundary/history paths currently fail closed, rather than making all ordinary near-goal paths unsupported.
