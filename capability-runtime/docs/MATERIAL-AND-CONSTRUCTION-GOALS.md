# Material and construction goals

`ensure_items(runtime, requirements, ...)` treats each requested count as a final inventory total, not an unverified production estimate. It selects supported ingredients from actual inventory and loaded resources, reserves the entire sibling ingredient bill and final requested totals, creates missing tools/workstations when supported, and re-approaches the same observed station after gathering. Each nested mutation retains the original deadline and shared action allowance. Search stays within newly observed dry loaded frontiers; unknown terrain and unavailable resource routes remain blocked.

Production uses the existing recipes plus exact recipe resources extracted from the pinned official Minecraft1.21.1 client JAR. The bundled JSON records source hashes. Fixed grid arrangements for shapeless blaze-powder and Ender-eye recipes are valid ordinary crafting arrangements; output identity/count is still verified from actual menu changes. Building stairs/slabs/doors/fences/panes, chests, bow and arrows have explicit vanilla recipes/counts. The vocabulary is finite. Unknown/modded recipes, occupied machines, nonempty cursors and uncertain actions are never cleaned up or replayed automatically.

For pearl, blaze-rod, string and gunpowder deficits, the material goal delegates to the species-specific mob-drop goal. A kill does not guarantee a drop. Equipment, safe combat stations, biome/structure availability and drop randomness remain real prerequisites; the goal does not fabricate resources or force spawn locations.

`build_with_materials(runtime, blueprint, job_id=..., ...)` accepts the exact schema3 blueprint. It derives the item bill (one item per two-cell door), checks all36 storage slots while preserving unrelated items, protects the declared construction region and support/access shell, and chooses an outside procurement worksite. Historical survey facts propose that site; movement and every mutation require new local observations. Workstation placement and mining cannot alter protected positions. The goal obtains the full material bill, returns to the original anchor, and calls the real oriented builder and its fresh local audit-window verification.

This is not inventory staging. A bill that cannot conservatively fit the available storage blocks before procurement; transient recipe capacity remains checked at every production step. A prior composition or building checkpoint prevents new procurement. Explicit building reconciliation/recovery is separate, and unknown actions keep their original IDs. No design approval, house geometry or terrain is inferred by this capability.

Offline composition tests run real recursive recipe/menu algorithms and the real building executor against synthetic inventories/world responses. They are not a live house build or proof that the user's original approved design has been imported.
