# Combat loop .6: bounded detours and conservative threat interruption

Version `1.1.7-combat-loop.6`. Isolated candidate based on verified `.5` archive SHA-256 `4576f26d08f742d7fb0ad05d8b3b5a51b9664f2a6dae075ac3bd248a68813244`. Not deployed, restarted, or tested in Minecraft by this work. No game/IPC, backup, automation, or desktop control was used. This is **not completed multi-mob combat support**.

## Motivation and scope

The completed `.5` evidence reports diamond skeleton 3/3 and iron skeleton 0/3; iron targets ended at 8, 8, and 2 health. The iron stops included two actual missing-support cells and one oak-log obstruction. These are equipment-group observations, not a causal armor comparison. A later retained terrain frame proves a geometrically plausible dry path beside the tree, not a successful live detour. Neither retained pit frame proves a safe route to reengage the lower lake-edge target. Missing support, fluid, unknown cells, hazards, and collision remain refusals.

Native `combat_entity` still owns exactly the requested entity ID/UUID/type and original world/player/session/action/deadline. It does not choose another attack target. New nearby danger overrides offense by stopping it; it does not silently switch attacks. Boats, pearls, flight, Enderman attacks, shulkers, and dragon combat are outside this candidate.

## Bounded local routes

`CombatDetour` searches small same-height routes. It has at most 3 planning attempts for the original action, 32 candidate points, 8 local goals, 512 route evaluations, 192 directed edge checks, and an 8 ms **cooperative** planning deadline. Routes have at most 2 intermediate points plus a local goal, radius 3 blocks, and edges at most 2 blocks. Each complete route must pass all terrain, entity, and non-target threat checks. Local goals make at least .4 block of target-distance progress and retain at least 1.3 blocks of target separation, with actual entity AABBs still authoritative. Intermediate points need not be closer to the target.

The engine reads only current client-loaded dry full-support flat terrain. Every route edge is divided into at most .65-block continuous-square checks. `FlatStepCorridor` remains byte-identical to `.5`; no support/fluid/hazard/unknown/collision exclusion was removed. Same-tick cells may be reused in a bounded 1,024-cell map, which is discarded each tick. The borrowed concepts from existing navigation are documented in `client-mod/docs/combat-detour-planner.md`; no external Python path executor or ordinary `follow_path` behavior was changed.

A successful plan is not movement permission. Every tick starts with a fresh threat/entity snapshot and checks the intended next swept segment again. Dynamic AABBs include nearby living entities and collidable nonliving entities. A new obstruction consumes another bounded plan attempt or ends `no_safe_route_*`. The original target, deadline, 12-block origin boundary, pursuit cumulative no-closure budget, and plan attempts are never renewed by a route, recovery, or failure. Recovery still has its original 20-tick/two-settled-sample limit. Landing now certifies the occupied stationary footprint; movement after landing must independently pass its current direct or detour corridor.

New route turns settle before changing movement direction. The pure motion gate requires finite grounded integer-height motion; first movement or a significant turn requires horizontal speed <=.03, continuation requires speed <=.35, perpendicular speed <=.03, no meaningful reverse motion, and |vertical speed| <=.08. Detours may request zero forward while settling, at most 10 consecutive and 40 cumulative samples per original action. These waits do not refund pursuit/deadline budgets. Urgent withdrawal refuses unready motion instead of claiming a safe wait near danger.

New-route live movement also checks all four (+/−.04,+/−.04) shifted .31-square continuous sweeps and AABBs. Their union covers a conservative .35-half-width footprint, rather than the old diagonal broad-phase rectangle. Future server impulses or new entities after the decision cannot be predicted. The check is local client evidence, not a physics proof or a survival guarantee. Ordinary pursuit before any detour retains the `.5` intended-step movement policy; its general knockback-prediction limitation was not claimed solved here.

The planner is intentionally incomplete. A full .65 live lookahead can reject a tight waypoint corner before the player reaches it; no unsafe clamping/input-distance promise was added. Candidate routes use the same .35 envelope as new-route live movement; later real pose changes can still invalidate them. Cold JVM class initialization/JIT and individual callbacks cannot be preempted; the 8 ms deadline is not hard real-time. An isolated synthetic cold-process probe found 99/100 routes, with the first attempt safely timing out; this is not game performance evidence. A timeout is reported, never hidden with automatic retries or reset budgets.

## Threat and damage safety

Before target-facing aim, recovery waiting, movement, or attack, a bounded snapshot examines at most 1,024 loaded/renderable entities and records at most 128 nearby living entities / 128 dynamic obstacles within 16 blocks. Enumeration has a 4 ms cooperative budget. Unknown, malformed, duplicate-identity, truncated, wrong-tick, or >150 ms-old snapshots fail closed. This is the client's loaded local neighborhood, not server-complete global knowledge or proof of no unseen enemies.

The concepts are separated:
- Observable potential threats include supported enemies, creepers, spiders, Endermen, other known hostile types, and actual Enemy/NeutralMob instances.
- Allowed attack types remain zombie, husk, zombie villager, skeleton, stray, and bogged.
- Allowed incidental sword-sweep targets are an empty set: every other living entity is protected, including another supported attacker and creepers.
- A creeper request is withdrawal-only request admission, never an attack grant.

Within the conservative 8-block body-clearance warning range, creepers, unsupported threats, multiple threats, or any non-target danger within the warning range interrupt offense. Any observed Enderman anywhere in the 16-block snapshot interrupts before target-facing aim. Every withdrawal tick independently refuses Enderman presence, including when an earlier health or creeper reason was already latched. No gaze-aware Enderman escape planner is claimed. Unknown/custom Enemy types and neutral mobs are conservatively treated as potential threats.

Health <=14 or accumulated positive health losses >=4 within one second latches interruption. Healing does not refund losses. The fixed 24-slot monitor refuses invalid data, sample/clock regression, long observation gaps, or capacity uncertainty. These are uncalibrated candidate thresholds, not demonstrated survival limits. The health monitor and safety latch persist through recovery and route replanning. Both axes and swords receive the same final snapshot-age gate immediately before attack dispatch.

A safety interruption never resumes offense in that action. On a fresh supported flat pose, the candidate tries up to 16 straight 3-block withdrawal rays, each fully checked in two <=1.5-block edges. A ray must not approach any observed dangerous entity along its entire segment and must improve nearest-threat endpoint clearance. Runtime motion, terrain, entities, threats, and envelope are checked again each tick. Withdrawal is limited to 60 ticks, 3 seconds, 4 blocks observed travel, and the unchanged original deadline/12-block area. Enderman, unknown/truncated/stale observation, unready momentum, unsupported pose, no route, new obstruction, or any bound ends in `risk_remaining`. Even completing the planned 3-block segment reports `risk_remaining`; it is never labeled safe disengagement or combat success.

No client swell flag is interpreted as a server fuse timer. No creeper melee is enabled. Current shield pose does not prove an arrow was blocked. Attack dispatches do not prove server hits. Health changes retain `damage_attribution: unknown`, and are not attributed to the nearest enemy or automatically to the player. The bounded `ClientboundDamageEventPacket` evidence ring is explicitly deferred to a separate change; it must retain source entity IDs/type/position and unknown associations because that packet has no damage amount.

## Outcomes and evidence

`outcome_kind` distinguishes `replanning`, `blocked`, `no_safe_route`, `attack_completed`, and `risk_remaining`. `risk_remaining` and `safety_assured:false` avoid equating input release with safety. A target-death observation can mark this single target attack completed while other observed risk remains. The legacy outer `target_dead_observed` success reason is retained for compatibility; it does not become a whole-encounter victory.

New evidence includes current threat snapshot tick/age/scope/completeness/entity identities, current gate reason/emergency identity, health/loss-window/sample evidence, latched interruption reason, sweep denial, route attempts/waypoints/candidate/edge counts, route-motion settling counters, live corridor rejection cell/entity and envelope margin, and withdrawal endpoint/time/travel. `blocked_corridor` retains the exact original blocked sweep when planning changes subsequent diagnostics. Existing target motion, pursuit-window, shield request/observation, and dispatch evidence remain. Some route evidence is hypothetical; `decision_pose` is the actual sampled player pose.

Advancing those scenes to competent engagement needs later strategy implementation and separate evidence.
